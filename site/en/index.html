Features

Four reasons to keep Voice Button in the tray.

One button covers what usually takes several different services.

01

Dictate anywhere

Click the tray microphone or press a hotkey and talk. The finished text can be inserted into the active window, taken from the clipboard, or found in the history.

02

Monologs from your phone

Your phone becomes a second microphone. Create named monologs and dictate into them while the desktop does the transcription; offline recordings wait on the phone.

03

Telegram bot

Send the bot a voice message or a file and get the transcript back.

04

Audio and video

Transcribe audio or video you already have — the file is processed on your computer.

How it works

Three steps. No cloud.

Dictation stays on your computer, so it does not need the internet.

  1. 01

    Click

    The tray microphone, a hotkey or the button in the window starts recording at once. The mouse button actions can be swapped.

  2. 02

    Speak

    Text grows while you talk. Long dictation is cut at pauses and transcribed piece by piece without waiting for you to stop.

  3. 03

    Take it

    The finished text is already on the clipboard, pasted at the caret, or waiting in the history with its date, time and source.

Phone

Speak into your phone — the text appears on your computer.

Turn the receiver on in the app and point your phone camera at the QR code. The phone opens a page of monologs — named conversations you dictate into like a chat.

  • One QR code and a one-time link; disconnect the phone with one button.
  • Monologs: a list of messages, editing, deleting and archiving.
  • With no connection, a recording stays on the phone and leaves later, stamped with the time it was actually spoken.
  • Transcription runs on the computer in the background and steps aside whenever you dictate yourself.
  • The original of every message stays in the history; edits on the phone live on their own.

Inside

A window that stays out of the way.

The app lives in the tray and the window opens when you need it: live capture, history and settings in one place.

History

Come back to any message.

Entries are grouped by day with their time and source. Open one to see the separate pieces of a long dictation, then copy it whole or piece by piece.

  • Search across the whole history
  • Copy in one click
  • Filter by source: you, server, file, phone, Telegram

Settings

Settings with no Save button.

Microphone, mouse actions, hotkeys, sounds, volume, language and autostart apply at once. A model can be downloaded or cancelled in the same window.

  • Microphone picker and recording sensitivity
  • Three global hotkeys: record, append and paste
  • Model downloads with progress and cancellation

Privacy

Recognition never leaves your computer.

Your voice is processed by models that run locally. There is no account and no subscription, and recordings never have to be sent to a server.

  • Models on your machine

    Whisper, GigaAM and SenseVoice run locally. You pick a model in the settings and download it once.

  • Audio stays only until the text is safe

    A recording is deleted as soon as its text is saved. If transcription failed, the audio stays on disk.

  • No account, no subscription

    No sign-up, no payments, no cloud processing. The source code is open under MIT.

Questions

The short answers.

If something is missing here, the README on GitHub has the rest.

Open the README
Does it need the internet?

Not for dictation: recognition runs on your computer. The internet is needed to download a model, and for the Telegram bot and phone access from another network.

Is it free?

Yes. The app and its source code are free under the MIT License. There is no subscription and no account.

Which languages are supported?

Multilingual Whisper and SenseVoice (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Cantonese) and Russian GigaAM. Whisper and SenseVoice detect the language themselves.

Can I dictate for a long time?

Yes. Speech is cut at pauses and transcribed as it goes: text grows while you talk, and after you stop only the last fragment is processed.

Where does the text go?

To the clipboard, into the active window in paste mode, or to the history. In the history an entry can be expanded and copied whole or piece by piece.
