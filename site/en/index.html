CAPTURE

You see the words
while you are still talking.

Transcription runs in parallel with recording: a long dictation is cut at pauses, and after you stop only the last fragment is processed. A ready file - audio or video - can be transcribed on the File tab; for a video the app takes the audio track itself.

01

From the tray or the window

Left click records a new message, middle click records and pastes at the cursor; the actions can be swapped. The icon shows recording and processing by colour.

02

Whisper and GigaAM

Pick multilingual Whisper, SenseVoice for Asian languages, or Russian GigaAM CTC/RNN-T. A model is downloaded only when you ask for it.

03

Quiet history

Days are separated by Today, Yesterday and weekday headers. Expand and copy past results; long lists are rendered lazily and stay responsive.

04

Private by design

Audio never leaves the machine and is deleted after transcription. If the text is not guaranteed, the file stays and opens right from the history. No account, no subscription, no cloud processing.

CONTROL WITHOUT FRICTION

Settings apply instantly.

No Save button. Microphone, mouse button actions, global hotkeys, sounds, volume, language and autostart change in place. If a model is still downloading, the selection applies once it is ready.

  • Microphone picker that avoids silent virtual cables, plus a sensitivity slider
  • Three global hotkeys: record, append and paste at the cursor
  • Download progress and cancellation
  • Automatic language detection where the model supports it
  • Rotating logs with a button that opens the folder

LOCAL API

Your voice inside any application.

Switch on the local HTTP server and stream audio into it from an editor, a script or a page. The app returns live and final text, and the computer's microphone is not needed — the client brings the sound.

  • PCM16 stream: live transcription and the final text
  • Per-session model override that leaves your settings alone
  • GET /api/help — the whole contract for agents and integrations
  • History shows who started the recording: you or the server

PHONE

Speak into your phone — the text appears here.

Turn the receiver on under External access → Phone and point the phone camera at the QR code. The phone opens a page where you can create monologs and dictate into them, while the transcription runs on the computer, in the background, and steps aside whenever you dictate yourself.

  • One phone, a one-time link, disconnect in one click
  • HTTPS: browsers hand over the microphone only in a secure context
  • Offline recordings stay on the phone and go out later, stamped with the time they were spoken
  • The original text stays in the history forever; edits on the phone live on their own
  • Internet access is two buttons: a firewall rule and, where the router allows it, a port mapping
