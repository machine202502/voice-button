# Bundled sound cues

All three cues are generated by the project itself: sine tones with two harmonics,
44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono, one envelope. No third-party recordings are used, so there is
nothing to attribute.

| File | Used for | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `start.wav` | start of recording | two rising notes, 660 → 880 Hz, 0.26 s |
| `end.wav` | text is ready | two falling notes, 880 → 660 Hz, 0.30 s |
| `error.wav` | the app recovered from a crash | three rising blips (880, 1046, 1318 Hz) and a low landing tone, 0.54 s |

The files are stored at their natural level; the app scales them at playback
(`sound_volume`, 50 % by default). Each cue can be turned off in Settings, and every
one has a **Play** button next to it.
